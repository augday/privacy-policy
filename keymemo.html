1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS application KeyMemo handles information related to you. This policy applies to users who download and use the app through the Apple App Store.

2. General Principles

  • No proprietary password cloud service: KeyMemo is designed primarily for local-first password management. Password data is stored on your device and may use Apple technologies such as Keychain and iCloud Keychain.
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs: The current version does not integrate advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs such as Firebase Analytics.
  • Data minimization: Information is processed only to the extent necessary for app functionality.

3. Information We Process

3.1 Password Vault Data

This may include account titles, usernames, passwords, notes, websites, categories, tags, and favorites that you create or import.

  • Purpose: To provide password management, search, editing, import/export, and security-related features.
  • Storage: Data may be stored locally using Apple Keychain services. If iCloud sync is enabled, synchronization may occur through Apple iCloud Keychain services.

3.2 Face ID and Touch ID

Face ID and Touch ID are used only for unlocking the app and authorizing sensitive actions.

Biometric information is processed by Apple on-device. KeyMemo does not access your original fingerprint or facial data.

3.3 Clipboard Usage

When you copy information from the app, content may be placed into the iOS clipboard.

If clipboard auto-clear is enabled, the app may attempt to clear clipboard content after a specified period.

3.4 Import and Export

Import and export features may involve local file access and sharing through Apple system services such as Files or AirDrop.

Please store exported files securely and only use trusted environments.

4. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not operate a web-based password hosting platform.
  • We do not sell password vault data.
  • We do not use password data for advertising profiles.

5. Apple Services

KeyMemo relies on Apple technologies including iOS, Keychain, iCloud, StoreKit, and LocalAuthentication.

Apple-related services are governed by Apple's own privacy policies.

6. Your Rights

You may edit or delete entries, disable iCloud sync, modify security settings, or uninstall the app at any time.

7. Security Disclaimer

No software can guarantee absolute security in all situations. Please also protect your device passcode, Apple ID, and system updates.

2. 总体原则

  • 无自有密码云： KeyMemo 采用本地优先设计， 数据主要保存在设备及 Apple 提供的 Keychain / iCloud Keychain 中。
  • 无广告与统计 SDK： 当前版本未集成广告、统计或崩溃分析 SDK。
  • 最小化原则： 仅处理实现功能所需的信息。

3. 我们处理的信息

3.1 密码库数据

包括账号、用户名、密码、网址、备注、分类、标签、收藏等内容。

  • 用途： 用于密码管理、搜索、编辑、导入导出与安全检查等功能。
  • 存储： 数据可能通过 Apple Keychain 保存在本地。 开启 iCloud 同步后， 数据可能通过 Apple iCloud Keychain 在设备间同步。

3.2 Face ID 与 Touch ID

Face ID 与 Touch ID 仅用于解锁应用及授权敏感操作。

生物识别数据由 Apple 在设备本地处理， KeyMemo 无法访问原始指纹或面容信息。

3.3 剪贴板

当您复制内容时， 信息可能写入 iOS 系统剪贴板。

若开启自动清除功能， 应用可能在指定时间后尝试清理剪贴板内容。

3.4 导入与导出

导入导出功能可能涉及本地文件访问与系统分享能力。

请妥善保管导出文件，并仅在可信环境中使用。

4. 我们不会做的事情

  • 不会运营网页版密码托管平台。
  • 不会出售密码库数据。
  • 不会使用密码数据进行广告画像。

5. Apple 服务说明

KeyMemo 使用 iOS、Keychain、iCloud、StoreKit、 LocalAuthentication 等 Apple 提供的系统能力。

Apple 服务受 Apple 自身隐私政策约束。

6. 您的权利

您可以随时修改、删除数据， 关闭 iCloud 同步， 或卸载应用。

7. 安全免责声明

任何软件都无法保证绝对安全。 请同时保护好设备密码、 Apple ID 与系统更新。
